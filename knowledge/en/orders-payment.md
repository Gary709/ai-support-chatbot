Payment is taken by the normal Shopify checkout, and a successful payment sends an email receipt. Do not put a card number in this chat.

If a payment failed, wait a minute and try the cart again. This chat cannot change a price or apply a private discount. A person can look at a failed payment if you open a ticket and say when you tried.
