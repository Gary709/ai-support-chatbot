This TPU is a firm 95A. TPU nozzle temperature is 220–240 °C and the bed is 40–60 °C. Print slowly, about 20–30 mm/s, with little retraction and a low fan. A direct-drive extruder is more reliable than a long Bowden tube.

Use more walls and less sparse infill on flexible parts. TPU takes on moisture quickly, so dry it before a long print and seal the bag afterwards.
