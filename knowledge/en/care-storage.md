title: Storing filament
tags: storage, dry, moisture, desiccant, canberra
---

Store filament sealed in its bag with the desiccant, even in Canberra. PETG and TPU pick up moisture faster than a dry room suggests. Popping, stringing, or a rough surface means the spool needs drying.

Dry PLA at about 45 °C, PETG at about 65 °C, and TPU at about 50 °C for several hours, and only if the dryer can hold that low temperature safely.
