The returns policy allows unopened spools to come back within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. Opened spools are not taken back for change of mind, because filament absorbs moisture. A damaged, badly tangled, or wrong-material spool can be replaced or refunded if you write within 7 days with the order number and a photo.

Colour shift between batches is normal and is not a defect. Keep PLA, PETG, and TPU sealed with the desiccant. This chat cannot start the refund itself; a person does that from a ticket if your case needs a judgement.
