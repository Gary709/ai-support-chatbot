Sea freight from China can take about six weeks, one and a half months, before a restock is on the shelf. How long it takes moves with ports and customs, so six weeks is the usual lead time, not a promised day. Incoming stock cannot ship until that batch lands. In-stock Canberra spools are not waiting on the sea leg.

Most filament is made for TinyFab 3D and sent by sea from China. The product page says In stock or Incoming. In stock ships from Canberra. Incoming means the batch is on the water or in customs. If you need a part before that, choose a colour that is already in stock.
