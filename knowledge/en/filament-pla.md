title: PLA printer settings

PLA nozzle temperature is 190–220 °C. A reliable PLA bed temperature is 55 °C, inside a 50–60 °C bed range. Use the part fan after the first layer, and a print speed around 40–80 mm/s.

PLA is the easy filament. It softens around 55–60 °C, so leave it off hot-car and dishwasher parts. If the nozzle pops or the surface pits, the spool is wet and needs drying at about 45 °C.
