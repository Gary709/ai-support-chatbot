Orders outside Canberra ship within Australia and the postage is calculated at checkout. This store does not ship outside Australia.

Capital cities are faster than remote addresses and PO boxes. Interstate parcels leave once the spool is in stock. An Incoming spool still has to finish the sea leg before any Australian address can receive it.
