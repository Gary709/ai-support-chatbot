Most local orders arrive the same day or the next business day. Same-day cut-off is 9:00am Monday to Friday and 12:00pm on weekends. TinyFab's own Canberra team delivers. Estimates aren't guaranteed.
For the rest of Australia, orders are dispatched in 1–3 business days, then delivery usually takes 1–8 business days.
For PO Boxes and Parcel Lockers, choose Australia Post. A tracking email is sent on dispatch.
Local delivery can take the same day or the next business day. Interstate delivery takes 1–8 business days after dispatch.
If a parcel is marked delivered but missing, or it arrives damaged, email support@tinyfab3d.com.au.
Shipping policy: https://www.tinyfab3d.com.au/policies/shipping-policy
