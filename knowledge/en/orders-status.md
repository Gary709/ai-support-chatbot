What does each order status mean, and how do you check an order status? Read the confirmation email. Placed means we have the order. Processing means it is being packed from Canberra stock. Incoming means the spool is still on a sea-freight batch and has not landed. Shipped means the courier has it. Delivered means it arrived.

This desk cannot open a live Shopify order, so a specific order number is handed to a person. The email receipt is the status record you already have.
