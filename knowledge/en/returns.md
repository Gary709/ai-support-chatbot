You can return within 30 days of receiving the order. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and come with proof of purchase.
To start a return, email support@tinyfab3d.com.au with the order number and the reason. If it's approved, TinyFab sends a return label and instructions. Items sent back without approval can't be accepted.
Change of mind: shipping both ways and payment processing fees aren't refunded, and approved returns have a 20% restocking fee. Sale items can't be returned for change of mind.
Damaged, defective or wrong items: contact TinyFab within 14 days of delivery with details and photos, and keep the remaining filament and the packaging. A burst vacuum bag doesn't count as a defect. If testing finds no fault, the claim may be treated as a change-of-mind return.
Nothing in the policy limits rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
Refund policy: https://www.tinyfab3d.com.au/policies/refund-policy
