title: Choosing PLA, PETG, or TPU
tags: pla, petg, tpu, filament, material, choose

If you are unsure which filament you should use, start with PLA. PETG is the tougher choice when a part needs more heat resistance. TPU is the flexible one. All three are sold as 1 kg spools of 1.75 mm filament.

PLA suits models, prototypes, and indoor parts that stay cool. PETG suits brackets and fixtures. TPU suits bumpers, grommets, and cases, and it has to be printed slowly. The setting notes for each material are separate.
