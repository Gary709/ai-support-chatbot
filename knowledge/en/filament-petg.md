title: PETG printer settings

PETG nozzle temperature is 230–250 °C, and the PETG bed temperature is 70–85 °C. Keep the fan low, about 0–30 percent, or the layers can split. A little stringing is normal.

Do not squash the first layer. PETG grips the bed and can pull a coating off. It copes with more heat than a typical indoor model needs. If the surface looks cloudy or the nozzle pops, dry the spool at about 65 °C.
