In-stock filament orders get free delivery in Canberra (ACT). Canberra orders leave local stock, usually within two business days. Free delivery covers the Canberra metro area only.

TinyFab 3D packs in-stock spools from Canberra. When the courier collects the parcel you get a dispatch email. Free delivery does not cover the rest of Australia. If a colour is not in local stock, it waits on the sea-freight batch and is not a free same-week delivery.
