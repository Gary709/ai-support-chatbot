Postage is calculated by weight at checkout. These are the current rates for a 1–3 kg parcel, about one or two 1kg spools. Checkout shows the final price.
Aramex ATL is $12.50 to NSW, VIC and SA, $16.50 to QLD, TAS and WA, and $10.13 within the ACT. Postage to Melbourne uses the VIC rate, currently $12.50 on Aramex ATL.
AusPost Parcel Post is $20.25 to NSW, VIC, SA and NT. Only NT is AusPost-only.
Aramex ATL is no-signature, so loss or theft after delivery isn't covered.
